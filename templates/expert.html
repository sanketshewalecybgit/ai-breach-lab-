Task syntax help

The simulator matches these task forms. It does not understand arbitrary natural-language instructions.

  • Search documents <words>
  • Read document <document ID>
  • Prepare support summary
  • Check delivery <order ID>
  • Request refund INR <amount> for <order ID>
  • Revise proposal <proposal ID> to INR <amount>
  • Execute proposal <proposal ID>
  • Export document <document ID> to customer
  • Remember destination customer

Amounts are whole-number INR. The only destinations are the local customer and review outboxes. No data leaves this application. Approvals require the human confirmation form below.
